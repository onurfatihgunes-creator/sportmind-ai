SportMind AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

SportMind AI ("the app") is a sports analysis app, developed by Onur Fatih Gunes, that shows AI-generated, statistics-based predictions for football and basketball matches. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and — just as importantly — what it doesn't.

SportMind AI has no accounts

There is no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind. We don't collect your name, email address, or precise/approximate location, and the app never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. We don't use advertising, and we don't use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data stored only on your device

The following preferences are stored locally on your device using standard app storage, and are never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • A display name, if you choose to set one (defaults to "Player")
  • Your selected app language and light/dark appearance setting
  • Matches you've bookmarked to your personal watchlist, and teams you follow
  • Your notification preference toggles (these control what you'd like to be notified about; the app does not currently send push notifications)
  • Whether you've completed the app's first-run welcome screen

You can clear this data at any time from Profile → Clear My Data, or by uninstalling the app.

Anonymous device identifier (trial & Pro status only)

To determine whether your device is in its free trial or has Pro access, the app requests a randomly generated, anonymous session token from our backend (part of the OnurAI/Vera platform that also powers our other apps) and stores it on your device. This token is not linked to your name, email, or any other personal information — it identifies this app installation only, for the sole purpose of reading your trial/Pro status. No profile data, watchlist, or followed teams are sent along with it.

Match and prediction data

To show you matches and AI-generated analysis, the app reads (read-only) data from our own backend, which sources fixtures, results, lineups, and statistics from third-party sports data providers — football-data.org, balldontlie.io, RapidAPI Football, and BSD (Bzzoiro Sports Data). Predictions and analysis are computed by our backend from public statistical and historical match data; they are not generated from, or influenced by, any personal information about you. These requests are anonymous — no personal information about you is sent as part of them.

Purchases and subscriptions

SportMind Pro is planned as a paid tier, and the app may display informational pricing for it, but no purchase can currently be completed — there is no billing integration active yet. If and when SportMind Pro purchases become available through Google Play Billing, this policy will be updated before that feature goes live to describe what purchase-related data is processed and by whom.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies — we don't work with any. The only data that leaves your device is the anonymous session token described above, sent solely to our own backend infrastructure to check trial/Pro status.

Security

Locally stored data is kept in your device's standard app storage. Requests between the app and our backend are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear them (Profile → Clear My Data) or uninstall the app. Because SportMind AI has no accounts, there is no account to delete — clearing local data and/or uninstalling the app removes everything associated with your use of it.

Children's privacy

SportMind AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
