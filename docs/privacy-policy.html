SportMind AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

SportMind AI ("the app") is a sports analysis app, developed by Onur Fatih Gunes, that shows AI-generated, statistics-based predictions for football and basketball matches. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and — just as importantly — what it doesn't.

SportMind AI has no accounts

There is no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind. We don't collect your name, email address, or precise/approximate location, and the app never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. We don't use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK of our own. The app does show ads, served by Google AdMob — see "Advertising" below for exactly what that involves.

Data stored only on your device

The following preferences are stored locally on your device using standard app storage, and are never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • A display name, if you choose to set one (defaults to "Player")
  • Your selected app language and light/dark appearance setting
  • Matches you've bookmarked to your personal watchlist, and teams you follow
  • Your notification preference toggles (these control what you'd like to be notified about; the app does not currently send push notifications)
  • Whether you've completed the app's first-run welcome screen

Profile → Clear My Data removes your display name, watchlist, followed teams, language choice, appearance setting, notification toggles and the first-run flag. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app stores on the device.

Device identifiers

The app itself does not create, read or send any device identifier or account token, and our own database requests carry none. The one exception is the advertising SDK described below, which uses the Android advertising ID. Earlier pre-release test builds of the app could store a randomly generated anonymous token on the device; this version does not read that token or send it anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

SportMind AI is free and is supported by ads. The ads are served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. You will see a banner at the bottom of the Home screen and of a match analysis, and occasionally a full-screen ad after you close a match analysis. We limit the ad content level to general audiences, and we do not sell your personal data.

To show and measure ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app sends Google information from your device, which can include: your Android advertising ID, your IP address (and the approximate location derived from it), device and operating system details, the app version, and how you interact with ads (such as views and taps), together with diagnostic information about how the SDK performs. Google uses this to serve and measure ads, to prevent fraud and abuse, and under its own privacy policy. We do not receive this data about you, and the ads do not read your watchlist, followed teams or any other information stored in the app.

Your choices. If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the app shows Google's consent form the first time it can show ads, and no ads are requested unless the form's result allows it. You can change that choice later in Profile (the "Ad privacy options" entry appears wherever the law requires it). On any device you can also reset or delete the advertising ID, and opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings (Google → Ads). If you do, ads continue but are not personalised.

More information: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Match and prediction data

To show you matches and AI-generated analysis, the app reads data (read-only) from our own database, which is hosted by Supabase. The requests carry no name, account or device identifier. Like any internet service, the database host necessarily receives the technical details of a request, such as the IP address and time. The data in that database — fixtures, results, lineups and statistics — is gathered by our backend from third-party sports data providers (football-data.org, balldontlie.io, RapidAPI Football and BSD / Bzzoiro Sports Data). Those providers are contacted only by our backend, never by the app, and they receive nothing about app users. Predictions and analysis are computed from public statistical and historical match data; they are not generated from, or influenced by, any personal information about you.

Links that open outside the app

The Legal screen links to this policy and our Terms of Service, which are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening them uses your device's browser, and GitHub receives the request like any website you visit. The app does not otherwise load content from third-party sites.

Purchases

SportMind AI is free to use. The app has no purchase, subscription or payment feature and collects no payment or purchase information (ads are the only way the app is funded). If paid features are ever offered, this policy will be updated before they become available to describe what is processed and by whom.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Apart from Google AdMob (described under "Advertising"), the app sends nothing about you to any party other than the connection details that reaching our database and the policy pages necessarily involve, as described above. We don't work with any analytics company.

Security

Locally stored data is kept in your device's standard app storage. Requests between the app and our backend are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear them (Profile → Clear My Data) or uninstall the app. Because SportMind AI has no accounts, there is no account to delete — clearing local data and uninstalling the app removes everything the app stores about your use of it.

Children's privacy

SportMind AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 13. Because the app shows ads, it is intended for users aged 13 and over.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
