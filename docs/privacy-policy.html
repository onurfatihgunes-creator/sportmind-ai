SportMind AI

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

SportMind AI ("the app") is a sports analysis app, developed by Onur Fatih Gunes, that shows AI-generated, statistics-based predictions for football and basketball matches. This policy explains what data the app handles, why, and — just as importantly — what it doesn't.

SportMind AI has no accounts

There is no sign-up, login, or user account of any kind. We don't collect your name, email address, or precise/approximate location, and the app never requests access to your camera, microphone, contacts, or photos. We don't use advertising, and we don't use any analytics or crash-reporting SDK.

Data stored only on your device

The following preferences are stored locally on your device using standard app storage, and are never transmitted to us or anyone else:

  • A display name, if you choose to set one (defaults to "Player")
  • Your selected app language and light/dark appearance setting
  • Matches you've bookmarked to your personal watchlist, and teams you follow
  • Your notification preference toggles (these control what you'd like to be notified about; the app does not currently send push notifications)
  • Whether you've completed the app's first-run welcome screen

Profile → Clear My Data removes your display name, watchlist, followed teams, language choice and notification toggles. Your appearance setting and the first-run flag are removed when you uninstall the app.

No device identifier is sent

The app does not create, read or send any device identifier, advertising ID or account token. Earlier pre-release test builds of the app could store a randomly generated anonymous token on the device; this version does not read that token or send it anywhere, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

Match and prediction data

To show you matches and AI-generated analysis, the app reads data (read-only) from our own database, which is hosted by Supabase. The requests carry no name, account or device identifier. Like any internet service, the database host necessarily receives the technical details of a request, such as the IP address and time. The data in that database — fixtures, results, lineups and statistics — is gathered by our backend from third-party sports data providers (football-data.org, balldontlie.io, RapidAPI Football and BSD / Bzzoiro Sports Data). Those providers are contacted only by our backend, never by the app, and they receive nothing about app users. Predictions and analysis are computed from public statistical and historical match data; they are not generated from, or influenced by, any personal information about you.

Links that open outside the app

The Legal screen links to this policy and our Terms of Service, which are hosted on GitHub Pages. Opening them uses your device's browser, and GitHub receives the request like any website you visit. The app does not otherwise load content from third-party sites.

Purchases

SportMind AI is free to use. The app has no purchase, subscription or payment feature and collects no payment or purchase information. If paid features are ever offered, this policy will be updated before they become available to describe what is processed and by whom.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or analytics companies — we don't work with any. The app sends nothing about you to any party other than the connection details that reaching our database and the policy pages necessarily involve, as described above.

Security

Locally stored data is kept in your device's standard app storage. Requests between the app and our backend are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections.

Data retention and deletion

Locally stored preferences remain on your device until you clear them (Profile → Clear My Data) or uninstall the app. Because SportMind AI has no accounts, there is no account to delete — clearing local data and uninstalling the app removes everything the app stores about your use of it.

Children's privacy

SportMind AI is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
