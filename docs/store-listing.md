## Full description (max ~4000 chars)

SportMind AI analyses upcoming football and NBA basketball matches from real statistical data —
recent form and expected goals or points — and shows the reasoning behind every estimate.

This is **not** a betting app. SportMind AI doesn't accept wagers, doesn't show odds and doesn't
facilitate gambling. Every analysis is a statistical likelihood, not a guarantee, and says so.

**What you get**
- Win-probability estimates and an outcome distribution for upcoming matches in the Premier League,
  La Liga, Serie A, Bundesliga, Ligue 1, Champions League, Süper Lig, EFL Championship, MLS and
  Brasileirão Série A, plus NBA games
- Home highlights: the day's matches with enough data to analyse, and the one with the clearest
  single driver
- Explore: browse the coming week's matches by day, search by team or league, filter by competition
- For each match: expected goals/points, recent form, a "Reasons" view comparing the two sides factor
  by factor, a "Change" view of what moved the estimate, and a short SportMind View
- Team pages with recent form and upcoming matches, AI Insights for a team (form, attack and defence
  trend, squad status), and a side-by-side team comparison
- Save matches and follow up to three teams; everything you save stays on your device
- Dark and light appearance; English, Turkish, German and Arabic with full right-to-left support

**Honest about its limits**
When a team has too little recent data, the app says so instead of guessing, and matches without
enough data are not recommended on the home screen. You can always see which factors drove an
estimate. Outcomes remain unpredictable.

SportMind AI is free to use. It has no accounts, no ads and no tracking.
